How to read this. Recall covers NUMERIC errors: quantity, unit 
price, total. Validation catches them because the three values 
contradict each other.

through any shortcoming of the implementation: nothing in the 
invoice reveals that 'Xetoprofen' should have read 'Ketoprofen'. 
Catching those would need a medication dictionary -- a separate 
and complementary avenue.
