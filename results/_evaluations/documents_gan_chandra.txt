GAN handwriting against a handwriting font, read by Chandra OCR 2
=================================================================

The paired measurement behind section 7.8 of docs/notion_final_report.md.

Same 20 specimens on both sides, same ground truth, same metric. The GAN
documents reuse the ground truth of their font-rendered twins, so the content
and the layout are identical and only the handwriting differs. That is what
makes the gap attributable to the handwriting itself.

                              handwritten fields ok    mean field score
  font (Playwrite VN)                     95.3 %               97.2
  GAN (WriteViT, 20 writers)              57.6 %               84.7
  ------------------------------------------------------------------
  cost of real handwriting              -37.7 pts             -12.5

Reproduce:

    python src/eval_handwriting.py --run documents_gan_chandra \
        --verite-dir results/documents_gan_chandra/verite --lang vi

    python src/eval_handwriting.py --run handwriting_vi_chandra \
        --verite-dir handwriting/generated --lang vi \
        --specimens specimen_000,...,specimen_019

The second command needs --specimens because the font run covers 30 documents
and this one covers 20. Scoring them over different pages would compare two
different test sets and the 37.7 points would mean nothing.

Two readings to carry with the number
-------------------------------------
The font specimens were far too easy. Every earlier handwriting figure in the
project rests on them, so they describe a best case that real handwriting does
not reach.

The true cost is larger than 37.7 points, not smaller. 14 % of the word
occurrences on these pages are digits, which the generator cannot draw and
which are therefore written with the font. Those are read under the easy
regime on both sides.
